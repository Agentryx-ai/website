Portfolio

Four products, one operating thesis.

Agentryx AI presents four company products: Agentryx, Itineva, ModuBoza, and ReTalk. Each product is labeled by its current posture so the public story stays accurate while the portfolio matures.

01 Core platform

Agentryx

A Codex-first Agent Company platform where users direct a shared Team Channel and the control plane routes work, coordinates agents, verifies completions, and keeps operational state.

  • Team Channel routing and agent wakeup
  • Task DAG, acceptance criteria, and approval flow
  • Deterministic checks plus semantic review
02 Product candidate

Itineva

An AI itinerary product that turns destination, dates, travelers, and preferences into shareable trip pages with versions, public gallery, route checks, and editable travel plans.

  • Itinerary generation and staged enrichment
  • Share links, gallery, import, and attribution
  • Places and route-grounded planning workflows
03 Private PoC

ModuBoza

A private OTT subscription operations PoC exploring provider pools, isolated credentials, one-person session rules, account verification, queueing, and control-plane administration.

  • Provider account pool and session management
  • Credential isolation and audit-oriented controls
  • Legal, payment, and launch review required
04 Direction fixed

ReTalk / 리톡

A local-first desktop restore product for conversation records already present on a user's PC, built around consent, profile selection, preview, paid unlock, search, and export.

  • Personal content remains local by default
  • Preview, unlock, restore progress, and room browsing
  • Export/reporting for backup or lawful submission

Operating thesis

Products first, claims second.

01

Control planes for messy work

The portfolio favors systems that track state, approvals, routing, versions, and evidence instead of one-off prompts.

02

Local-first when data is personal

ReTalk's launch direction keeps conversation content on the user's machine by default and treats server identity as licensing, not data custody.

03

Private PoCs stay labeled

Products with legal, payment, platform, or operational review still appear in the portfolio, but the site does not present them as launched services.
